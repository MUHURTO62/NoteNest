Our Mission

To empower CSE students by providing a centralized, free, and organized platform for accessing academic resources, previous year questions, and shared knowledge - making quality education accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

To become the largest student-driven academic platform in Bangladesh where every CSE student of IIUC can find, share, and contribute to educational resources, fostering a collaborative learning community.
